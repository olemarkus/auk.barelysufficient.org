Control your Auk indoor garden from Homey. The app talks to the garden directly over Bluetooth, so it needs an Auk with Bluetooth, such as Auk Mini 2.

Turn the light on or off, dim it, or let it follow the growth mode. Switch growth mode between Herbs & salads, Fruits & veggies and Slow, and choose what time the light comes on each day.

You can control the garden from the device in Homey or from Flows.
